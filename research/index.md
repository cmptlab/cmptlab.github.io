# Research

## Tumor immunometabolism

How do shared metabolic pathways in tumor and immune cells shape antitumor immunity? We study glycolysis, glutamine utilization, and pyrimidine biosynthesis in the tumor microenvironment. We design immune–metabolic drug conjugates (IMDC) and related chemical tools to investigate how these pathways can be modulated in specific cell populations.

## Cancer cachexia

How does a tumor alter metabolism in distant tissues? We investigate tumor–host communication and the immune and metabolic changes that contribute to skeletal muscle and adipose tissue loss.

## Sepsis and organ injury

How does immune-cell metabolism influence inflammatory injury and recovery during sepsis? We study macrophage responses and develop drug delivery strategies to modulate these responses.
