<section markdown="0"><h1>Research</h1>
<p>Our laboratory investigates the interplay between <strong>cellular metabolism and immune regulation</strong> in major human diseases, including cancer, sepsis, and cancer cachexia. Our research focuses on key metabolic pathways, particularly <strong>glucose metabolism, pyrimidine metabolism, and glutamine metabolism</strong>, and their roles in regulating cellular function, immune responses, and disease progression. Understanding these complex interactions provides new opportunities for developing innovative therapeutic strategies.</p>
<p>However, metabolic pathways are often shared by diseased cells and healthy tissues, making selective therapeutic intervention a major challenge. We aim to address this challenge by integrating <strong>mechanistic studies of immunometabolism and innovative drug design</strong> to develop more precise and effective therapeutic approaches.</p>
<p>Keywords: <em>Immunometabolism, cancer, sepsis, cancer cachexia.</em></p></section>

<section markdown="0"><h2>Immunometabolic Mechanisms</h2>
<p>Cellular metabolism plays a critical role in regulating immune-cell function and disease progression. We investigate how <strong>metabolic reprogramming shapes immune responses and cellular interactions</strong> in cancer, sepsis, and cancer cachexia, with a particular focus on the interplay between metabolic pathways and immune regulation.</p>
<p>Our research aims to identify key metabolic pathways and molecular mechanisms that govern immune-cell activation, inflammatory responses, and disease-associated metabolic dysfunction. These studies provide a mechanistic foundation for identifying novel therapeutic targets and developing targeted immunometabolic interventions.</p>
<p>Keywords: <em>Cellular metabolism, metabolic reprogramming, Immunometabolism, macrophage biology, inflammation.</em></p></section>

<section markdown="0"><h2>Innovative Drug Design</h2>
<p>Targeting cellular metabolism offers promising therapeutic opportunities, but the widespread utilization of metabolic pathways across different cell populations presents major challenges for drug selectivity and therapeutic efficacy.</p>
<p>We develop <strong>innovative therapeutic agents</strong> to modulate disease-associated metabolic and immune pathways. A major focus of our research is the development of <strong>immune–metabolic drug conjugates (IMDC)</strong>, which integrate metabolic intervention and immune regulation within coordinated therapeutic strategies.</p>
<p>We also explore <strong>bioorthogonal chemistry, proteolysis-targeting chimeras (PROTAC), and immunometabolic nanomedicines</strong> to advance targeted therapeutic interventions, with the goal of improving therapeutic precision and efficacy while minimizing unwanted effects on healthy tissues.</p>
<p>Keywords: <em>medicinal chemistry, Immune–metabolic drug conjugates (IMDC), prodrugs, PROTAC, bioorthogonal chemistry, nanomedicine.</em></p></section>
