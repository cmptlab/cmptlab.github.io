---
title: "Endogenous metabolite nanomedicine for septic lung injury"
layout: news_page
group: news
date: 2026-11-18
---

Our study in *Journal of Nanobiotechnology* introduces HSLPs, a liposomal nanomedicine that co-delivers two endogenous metabolites, 5-hydroxyindoleacetic acid (5-HIAA) and spermine. In mouse models of sepsis, HSLPs reduced lung inflammation and preserved pulmonary sensory nerve integrity, highlighting the potential of targeting both macrophage responses and sensory nerve dysfunction in septic lung injury.

Published online on 17 November 2026.
